First Aid Certified course guide 2027

First Aid Certified provides nationally recognised first aid and workplace safety training across Perth, Western Australia, at three training centres: Subiaco, Innaloo and Joondalup. Classes run seven days a week, including weekends and selected evenings. Training is for individuals, businesses, healthcare workers, childcare educators, disability support workers and other industries. Most short courses are delivered in partnership with ABC First Aid (RTO 3399). The Certificate III in Individual Support is delivered with Safety Corp. Contact: hello@firstaidcert.com.au.

Which course do I need?

Childcare, early learning, OSHC, family day care or school educators: Childcare First Aid (Provide First Aid in an Education and Care Setting, HLTAID012). It already includes CPR, Basic Emergency Life Support and general First Aid, so they do not need to book those separately.

Most workplaces, a first aid officer, sport coaches, fitness, hospitality, retail, office, security, mining and general work requirements: First Aid & CPR (Provide First Aid, HLTAID011). It includes CPR and Basic Emergency Life Support.

Only need a CPR certificate, or a yearly CPR refresher (nurses, healthcare workers, lifeguards, many workplaces): CPR (Provide Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation, HLTAID009).

Aged care, disability support, NDIS or home care workers who lift, move or support people: Manual Handling (Conduct Manual Tasks Safely, care environment, HLTWHS005). Many also need CPR or First Aid, which is cheaper as a Manual Handling combination.

Aged care or disability support workers who give medication to clients: Assist Clients with Medication (HLTHPS006).

Electricians, apprentices and anyone working near live low voltage panels: Low Voltage Rescue. It is only run together with CPR (Low Voltage Rescue + CPR) or with First Aid (First Aid + Low Voltage Rescue), never on its own.

Anyone working on a construction site: White Card (Prepare to Work Safely in the Construction Industry, CPCWHS1001).

Childcare, school and youth workers who need mandatory reporting training: Child Protection (Identify and Report Children and Young People at Risk, CHCPRT025). This is NOT a first aid course; it is fully online.

People wanting to start a career in aged care or disability support: Certificate III in Individual Support (Ageing and Disability, CHC33021).

Groups of staff or training at the workplace: corporate and onsite training is available; the team quotes it.

Express and Standard courses

First Aid & CPR, CPR and Childcare First Aid each come as Express or Standard. Both give exactly the same nationally recognised qualification and certificate, cost the same, and require the same assessments. Express combines online learning with a shorter face-to-face practical session. Standard gives additional classroom time for trainer explanations, demonstrations, questions, hands-on practice and revision. Both suit first-time students, people renewing, healthcare workers, childcare educators and disability support workers; no previous first aid or CPR experience is needed for Express. The words Express and Standard do not appear on the certificate. Express is not a lower qualification and Express students do not skip assessments.

First Aid & CPR (Provide First Aid, HLTAID011)

Includes the units HLTAID009 Provide CPR, HLTAID010 Provide Basic Emergency Life Support and HLTAID011 Provide First Aid. Express: about 1 hour of online learning and up to about 1.5 hours face-to-face. Standard: about 1 hour of online learning and up to about 3 hours face-to-face. Price $95 for either version for courses from 4 January 2027 ($85 for sessions before then). Recommended to renew every three years, with CPR refreshed every year.

CPR (Provide Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation, HLTAID009)

Express: about 20 minutes of online learning and about 1 hour face-to-face. Standard: about 20 minutes of online learning and up to about 1.5 hours face-to-face. Price $45 for either version for courses from 4 January 2027 ($39 for sessions before then). CPR refresher training is generally recommended every 12 months.

Childcare First Aid (Provide First Aid in an Education and Care Setting, HLTAID012)

Includes HLTAID009 CPR, HLTAID010 Basic Emergency Life Support, HLTAID011 Provide First Aid and HLTAID012. Express: about 45 to 60 minutes of online learning and up to about 1.5 hours face-to-face. Standard: about 45 to 60 minutes of online learning and up to about 3 hours face-to-face. Price $99 for either version for courses from 4 January 2027 ($95 for sessions before then).

Manual Handling (Conduct Manual Tasks Safely, care environment, HLTWHS005)

Online learning plus about 1 hour of face-to-face practical training, focused on safe manual tasks in care environments. Suits aged care, disability support, healthcare and community support workers. Price $99.

Manual Handling combinations (discounted packages)

CPR + Manual Handling (HLTAID009 and HLTWHS005): $129 as one booking. Booked separately from 4 January 2027 they would cost $144, a saving of $15.

First Aid & CPR + Manual Handling (HLTAID011 and HLTWHS005, which covers CPR, Basic Emergency Life Support, First Aid and Manual Handling): $169 as one booking. Booked separately from 4 January 2027 they would cost $194, a saving of $25.

When someone needs both courses, the combination is the recommended booking. The combination price is a package price, not a coupon.

Low Voltage Rescue

Low Voltage Rescue + CPR (UETDRMP018 and HLTAID009): about 15 to 20 minutes of online learning and about 2 hours face-to-face. Price $130. First Aid + Low Voltage Rescue (UETDRMP018 and HLTAID011): about 45 to 60 minutes of online learning and about 3 hours face-to-face. Price $230. Sessions usually start at 8:15 am.

White Card (Prepare to Work Safely in the Construction Industry, CPCWHS1001)

Blended: up to about 2 hours of online learning plus about 1 hour of face-to-face practical training and assessment. It is never fully online and never face-to-face only. Complete the online learning before class to have the White Card issued on the same day. Students can still attend the practical if the online learning is not finished, but the card cannot be issued until everything is complete. Price $99.

Assist Clients with Medication (HLTHPS006)

About 1 hour of online learning, which must be completed before attending, and about 3 hours of face-to-face practical training. Price $249 for courses from 4 January 2027 ($199 for sessions before then). Selected sessions may show a discounted price on the booking page.

Child Protection (Identify and Report Children and Young People at Risk, CHCPRT025)

100% online and self-paced; no classroom attendance. Price $119 from 4 January 2027 ($99 before then). "Pending" on an assessment means it has been submitted and is ready for marking; assessments are normally marked within 24 hours of submission. Once all assessments are complete and the USI is valid, the certificate is normally issued within 24 hours, emailed and available in the student portal.

Certificate III in Individual Support (Ageing and Disability, CHC33021)

Delivered with Safety Corp. Combines face-to-face and online learning, indicatively about 9 weeks, indicative course fee about $2,999. Intake dates, places and final fees must be confirmed by the team. Course information: https://firstaidcert.com.au/certificate-iii-individual-support-perth/

Online learning before class

Assist Clients with Medication: online learning must be completed before the practical. White Card: complete it before class for a same-day card; attendance is still allowed if unfinished. All other blended courses (CPR, First Aid & CPR, Childcare First Aid in Express and Standard, Manual Handling, both combinations, both Low Voltage Rescue courses): online learning is strongly recommended beforehand, but students can attend and finish it afterwards. Certificates are only issued once all requirements are complete.

Certificates

Certificates are normally issued within 24 hours of successfully completing all course requirements: online learning, practical assessment, a valid and verified USI, correct enrolment details and no outstanding payment. They are emailed and available in the student portal at mytraininghub.com.au. If a certificate has not arrived, the team investigates.

Tax invoices and receipts

Students download their own tax invoice or receipt from the student portal at mytraininghub.com.au: log in, open My Courses, choose the course, scroll to the payment section and click the green Receipt button.

Changing a booking

At least 24 hours' notice is needed for booking changes. With less than 24 hours' notice a $25 rebooking fee applies. If the booking system remembered an earlier selection and a student booked the wrong date or location by mistake, it is corrected free of charge. The $25 fee is not a refund or cancellation fee. Refund requests go to the team. To request a change, students email or text their preferred new date, time and location.

USI

A Unique Student Identifier (USI) is required for nationally recognised training, and enrolment details must match the USI record. USI verification problems are handled by the team.

Corporate and onsite training

Group and onsite workplace training is available. Pricing is quoted by the team based on the course and number of participants.
