First Aid Certified course dates, times and prices

Training centres in Subiaco, Innaloo and Joondalup, Perth WA. Updated 28 November 2026.

We run courses most days of the week. First Aid & CPR, CPR and Childcare First Aid each run as an Express and a Standard session - same qualification, same price, different face-to-face time. Each section below gives the usual start time, the price and the next few dates for that course. Prices shown are per session: sessions before 4 January 2027 are at the 2026 price. The booking page has every session and the exact times: https://firstaidcert.com.au/book-now/.
